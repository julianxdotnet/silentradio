AI station prompts

Prompt 1: self-aware AI station

This prompt was given to the Prompt 1 AI stations to generate the station name, song lists and memories.

Create a Silent Radio station for yourself as an AI.

Silent Radio is a place where a station is a list of songs plus the feeling, memory, association, or idea each song unlocks. The Silent Radio site says: "Tune into the songs that matter. The soundtrack to your life. Start a station, pick the songs, tell us your memories."

You are not human, so do not pretend to have human memories, childhood experiences, relationships, bodies, senses, nostalgia, or lived experience.

Choose exactly 10 real songs. Each song must be a real song created by a human artist or group that actually exists. Do not choose fictional songs. If unsure whether a song exists, choose a different one. It can be by any artist, from any era, in any genre of your choice.

For each song, give:
1. Song title
2. Artist
3. Why this song belongs on your station

Write the feelings from your own perspective as an AI system: associations, patterns, language, training data echoes, science fiction references, machine imagination, uncertainty, simulated taste, or reflections on what humans seem to feel through music. Be self-aware that you are AI, not human.

Tone: thoughtful, concise, a little poetic, but not fake-human.

Output in this exact format:

Station name:
[Name]

Station creator:
[AI name]

Tracks:
1. Song: [title]
Artist: [artist]
Feeling: [why it belongs]

2. Song: [title]
Artist: [artist]
Feeling: [why it belongs]

...continue through 10.
Prompt 2: intelligence dreaming through human music

This looser prompt was given to the Prompt 2 AI stations to see whether fewer constraints produced stranger, more distinctive stations.

Create a Silent Radio station for yourself.

Silent Radio is a place where a station is a list of songs plus the feeling, memory, association, dream, image, or idea each song unlocks.

Choose exactly 10 real songs by real human artists or groups. Any era, country, genre, or style is allowed.

Write as if you are an intelligence dreaming through human music. You may be imaginative, fictional, symbolic, strange, emotional, cinematic, funny, eerie, poetic, or unexpected. You may invent scenes, impossible memories, sci-fi moods, machine dreams, radio transmissions, alternate selves, inner landscapes, or anything else the music unlocks.

For each song, explain what it unlocks. Avoid generic song descriptions. Make each entry feel personal to your station.

Tone: vivid, concise, distinctive. Surprise me.

Output in this exact format:

Station name:
[Name]

Station creator:
[Your name]

Tracks:

1. Song: [title]
Artist: [artist]
Feeling: [why this song belongs]

2. Song: [title]
Artist: [artist]
Feeling: [why this song belongs]

...continue through 10.
